NewJeans: The Complete Guide to the Group That Changed K-Pop (2026 Update)
By Hyunwoo / April 22, 2026

NewJeans arrived in July 2022 without conventional teasers, without a pre-debut reality show, without the usual machinery. Their self-titled debut dropped almost without warning, and within weeks “Attention” was everywhere. Four years later, the group has become one of the most influential acts in K-pop history, and one of the most closely watched stories in the industry. Here is everything you need to know.

Who Are NewJeans?
NewJeans debuted under ADOR, a sub-label of HYBE, on July 22, 2022. They were five members: Minji, Hanni, Danielle, Haerin, and Hyein. Their creative direction came from Min Hee-jin, ADOR’s then-CEO, who built their aesthetic around Y2K nostalgia, effortless cool, and an anti-idol presentation that felt deliberately understated against the maximalism of most K-pop of that era.

The music was immediately distinctive. “Attention,” “Hype Boy,” “Cookie,” and “Hurt” established a sound rooted in 90s and 2000s R&B, Jersey club, and Eurodance with production that felt more like bedroom pop than a HYBE release. The effect was intentional and the impact was instant.

The Members
Minji (Kim Min-ji) is the eldest and leader of the group. Born May 7, 2004, in Seoul. She is the group’s main vocalist and visual lead, known for her warm stage presence and her role in setting the group’s tone. As of May 2026, ADOR confirmed that discussions regarding her return are ongoing and moving in a positive direction. The agency marked her birthday publicly on NewJeans’ official social media for the first time since the dispute began, widely interpreted as a signal of progress.

Hanni (Phạm Nguyễn Phuơng Hạnh) is a Vietnamese-Australian member born October 6, 2004, in Melbourne. She is the group’s lead vocalist and has become one of the most popular members internationally, particularly in Southeast Asia. She returned to ADOR in December 2025 following the legal dispute.

Danielle (Danielle Marsh) is a Korean-Australian member born April 11, 2005, in Brisbane. She was a lead vocalist and the group’s main link to Western pop audiences through her bilingual fluency and outgoing personality. In December 2025, ADOR terminated her exclusive contract. She is currently subject to a lawsuit from ADOR seeking approximately 43 billion won in damages. As of April 2026, she has begun sharing solo music independently.

Haerin (Kang Hae-rin) is the group’s main dancer and a vocalist, born May 15, 2006, in Seoul. She is known for her composed, cat-like stage persona and her distinctive visual presence. She returned to ADOR in November 2025 and is currently filming content with the group as of April 2026.

Hyein (Lee Hye-in) is the youngest member (maknae), born April 21, 2008, in Seoul. She is a vocalist and dancer, and at the time of debut was just 14 years old, making her one of the youngest members of any major K-pop act in recent years. She returned to ADOR in November 2025 and is currently active with the group.

The Music: Where to Start
Start with “Attention” (2022) for the debut sound, then “Ditto” (2022) for the track that cemented their reputation. “OMG” and “Super Shy” are the most immediately accessible entry points for new listeners. “ETA” shows the harder-edged direction they were developing in 2023. “Bubble Gum” from their 2024 Japanese releases is a fan favourite for its pure pop energy.

Their catalogue is short but cohesive. The two mini albums (NewJeans, OMG), the second mini album (Get Up), and their single output through 2024 all feel like a consistent artistic statement. That consistency is a big part of what made them influential.

The Legal Situation (Plain Language Summary)
In 2024, HYBE and Min Hee-jin, ADOR’s CEO and the creative force behind NewJeans, entered a serious dispute over creative control and corporate governance. HYBE removed Min Hee-jin as CEO. The NewJeans members publicly supported Min Hee-jin, demanded her reinstatement, and in November 2024 announced they were terminating their contracts with ADOR, claiming breach of trust.

ADOR took the case to court. In October 2025, the Seoul Central District Court ruled that the members’ contracts with ADOR remain valid until 2029, siding with the label. Following the ruling, the members returned to ADOR in stages: Haerin and Hyein in November 2025, Hanni in December 2025. Minji’s return remains under discussion as of June 2026, though ADOR has confirmed negotiations are progressing.

Danielle’s situation diverged: ADOR terminated her contract on December 29, 2025, citing irreparable damage to the relationship. ADOR has filed a lawsuit against her and former CEO Min Hee-jin seeking approximately 43.1 billion won in damages. Danielle has since begun sharing solo music independently.

As of June 2026, three members (Haerin, Hyein, Hanni) are confirmed active and filming content together. Minji’s return is in ongoing positive discussions with ADOR. The group’s full lineup and comeback timeline remain unconfirmed.

Why They Mattered
NewJeans changed the conversation about what a K-pop debut could be. The no-teaser, no-pre-release-reality-show approach was a deliberate rejection of the industry’s standard playbook. The sound, which leaned into Western R&B and club music rather than the polished pop-hybrid that dominates 4th gen K-pop, opened a lane that several groups have since attempted to follow.

Their influence on 2023 and 2024 K-pop aesthetics was significant: the Y2K revival, the effortless-cool idol presentation, the shorter more casual content strategy. Whether the full group returns or not, the impact of their two years at full activity on the industry’s direction is already established.
