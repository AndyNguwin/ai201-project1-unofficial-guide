NewJeans Timeline: Every Major Update on the ADOR Situation (Updated June 2026)
By Hyunwoo / April 21, 2026

This page tracks confirmed developments in the NewJeans situation. Last updated: June 5, 2026.

Current Status (June 2026)
Haerin: Back at ADOR since November 2025. Confirmed filming content in Copenhagen, Denmark (April 14, 2026).

Hyein: Back at ADOR since November 2025. Confirmed filming content in Copenhagen, Denmark (April 14, 2026).

Hanni: Back at ADOR since December 2025. Confirmed present in Copenhagen alongside Haerin and Hyein (April 14, 2026).

Minji: Status unresolved as of June 2026. On May 7, 2026, ADOR confirmed to media that discussions are ongoing and “moving in a generally positive direction.” On the same date, ADOR posted a birthday celebration for Minji on NewJeans’ official social media accounts for the first time since the dispute began. No official confirmation of return or lineup decision has been made.

Danielle: Contract terminated by ADOR on December 29, 2025. Subject to a lawsuit from ADOR seeking approximately 43.1 billion won in damages. Began sharing solo music independently on April 11, 2026, posting a self-composed demo track on Instagram. Pursuing an independent career.

Timeline
May 2026
May 7: ADOR confirms to media that discussions with Minji regarding her future activities are “ongoing in a generally positive direction.” On the same day, ADOR posts a birthday celebration for Minji on NewJeans’ official social media accounts, the first time the group’s official account has publicly acknowledged Minji since the Min Hee-jin dispute. No official return announcement has been made.

April 2026
April 14: ADOR confirms Haerin, Hyein, and Hanni are in Copenhagen, Denmark, with production staff. Fans and industry observers interpret the overseas schedule as a signal toward resuming official activities.

April 11: Danielle posts a self-composed demo track on Instagram, described as an easygoing pop song. Widely interpreted as the beginning of her solo career following her exit from NewJeans.

March 2026
March 25: ADOR announces a NewJeans fan club membership update, prompting significant fan backlash given the unresolved status of Minji and the ongoing lawsuit against Danielle. The announcement is criticised as premature given the group’s incomplete lineup situation.

January 2026
January: Danielle opens a personal account on the Chinese social media platform Xiaohongshu, fuelling speculation about solo activity. She confirms via Instagram Live that she is pursuing activities but that “many things are still being sorted out.”

December 2025
December 29: ADOR officially terminates Danielle’s exclusive contract, citing irreparable damage to trust. Simultaneously announces a civil lawsuit against Danielle and former ADOR CEO Min Hee-jin seeking 43.1 billion won in damages.

Late December: Hanni confirms her return to ADOR following discussions. Three of the five original members are now back with the label.

November 2025
November 12: Haerin and Hyein officially confirm they will continue activities under ADOR following the court ruling. ADOR states it is in discussions with Minji, Hanni, and Danielle.

November: All five members signal intention to return to ADOR following the October court ruling, though ADOR states it is verifying the sincerity of each individual decision.

October 2025
October 30: Seoul Central District Court rules in favour of ADOR, confirming that the NewJeans members’ exclusive contracts remain valid until 2029. The group’s attempt to terminate contracts early is dismissed.

March 2025
March: Members announce a hiatus from activities in response to the court’s provisional injunction, which had been preventing them from conducting independent activities without ADOR approval. The group temporarily used the name NJZ during this period when attempting to act independently.

November 2024
November: NewJeans members announce they are terminating their exclusive contracts with ADOR, citing breach of trust following HYBE’s removal of Min Hee-jin as ADOR CEO. ADOR disputes the termination and files a lawsuit to confirm contract validity, along with a provisional injunction to prevent independent activities.

August 2024
August: HYBE removes Min Hee-jin as ADOR CEO following a corporate dispute over creative control, governance, and allegations of attempted management takeover. Min Hee-jin departs. The NewJeans members publicly demand her reinstatement.

July 2022
July 22: NewJeans debut under ADOR with their self-titled mini album. “Attention” is released with almost no conventional pre-debut promotion. The approach and the music immediately attract industry attention.

What Is Known and What Remains Unconfirmed
Three members (Haerin, Hyein, Hanni) are confirmed back with ADOR and filming new content. Minji’s return is the subject of ongoing discussions that ADOR has described as progressing positively, but no official announcement has been made. The group’s comeback lineup, whether three or four members, has not been confirmed by ADOR. A comeback in the second half of 2026 has been reported by financial analysts citing HYBE earnings forecasts, but ADOR has not officially confirmed a schedule.

Danielle’s situation is separate: her contract was terminated by ADOR in December 2025, and an ongoing civil lawsuit means any resolution there is a legal matter, not a lineup decision. She has begun pursuing an independent solo career.
