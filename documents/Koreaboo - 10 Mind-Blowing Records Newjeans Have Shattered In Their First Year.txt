10 Mind-Blowing Records Newjeans Have Shattered In Their First Year
Andrea Nolan
July 22nd, 2023
From MelOn to Spotify and Billboard, NewJeans have dominated every platform.

One can’t possibly capture the sheer extent of the milestones NewJeans have achieved in their debut year, but we’ve tried to compile a collection of their most notable feats. Their explosive arrival on the music scene has seen them breaking records and amassing an extensive fanbase since the release of their first-ever single, “Attention,” one year ago. Each of these records tells a tale of their massive impact and influence in their first year of debut.

1. Fastest K-Pop Act to Reach 1 Billion Spotify Streams
NewJeans has proven that their music transcends borders and resonates with listeners worldwide. Their eclectic and addictive tracks have quickly garnered them 1 billion Spotify streams, the fastest for any K-Pop act. This monumental achievement only attests to their far-reaching appeal and the global reach of their music.

2. First Act to Simultaneously Occupy the Top 3 in the History of MelOn 24hits
Charting on MelOn 24hits is a significant milestone in itself, but NewJeans didn’t stop there. They became the first act ever to dominate the entire Top 3 simultaneously, a testament to their pervasive influence in the music scene. The feat is not just an indication of their success but also of their ability to consistently deliver hit after hit.

3. Most Weeks at No.1 on Circle Digital Chart in History
NewJeans’ catchy single “Ditto” took the world by storm and has maintained its grip on the No.1 spot on the Circle Digital Chart longer than any other song. This record signifies their ability to captivate audiences for an extended period, keeping listeners hooked on their unique sound.

4. Most Days and Most Hours at #1 on MelOn
Further showcasing the irresistible appeal of “Ditto,” the track not only led the MelOn charts for the most days but also for the most hours among all K-Pop acts. Such long-lasting chart dominance underscores the enduring popularity of their music.

5. The Longest Charting Song on MelOn Weekly Chart Top 10 in History
With “Hype Boy,” Newjeans not only broke records but also made history. The song remained in the MelOn Weekly Chart Top 10 longer than any other song, further cementing the group’s place in the K-Pop annals.

6. The 4th Generation Group with Most Entries in Billboard Hot 100
International recognition came swiftly for NewJeans, with the group charting more songs on the Billboard Hot 100 than any other 4th generation idol group. This notable achievement underscores their music’s widespread acceptance beyond South Korea.

7. The Idol Group With the Most Cumulative Weeks on MelOn Top 10 in a Single Year
NewJeans has spent more weeks on the MelOn Top 10 than any other idol group in a single year. This record speaks volumes about the consistent quality of their releases and their ability to connect with listeners.

8. Most Monthly Listeners for a 4th Generation Group on Spotify
NewJeans smashed yet another Spotify record, boasting the highest number of monthly listeners for a 4th generation group. Their music’s universal appeal is evident in the substantial number of people tuning in each month.

9. Highest Peak for a 4th Generation Group Song on the Billboard Global 200
Scaling new heights on the Billboard Global 200, NewJeans hit No. 8, the highest peak for a 4th generation group song. This feat showcases their music’s international appeal and their growing global footprint.

10. The 4th Generation Group with the Most “Real Time All-Kills”
Rounding out their impressive year, NewJeans amassed the most Real Time All Kills (RAKs) of any 4th Gen group, reaching #1 on all real-time charts like Melon, Bugs, Genie, etc. This record epitomizes the group’s immense popularity and their ability to resonate with fans across multiple platforms.
